Privacy & trust

Your data, your keys, your send button.

A job search is personal. Vanilla is built so you never have to take its word for it.

Read the privacy policy →
  • Keys encrypted with AES-256-GCM

FAQ

Good questions.

Is Vanilla free?

Yes. You connect your own AI provider key, so any AI usage is billed to you by that provider, not by us. Google's Gemini and OpenRouter both have free tiers with no card needed, which is the easiest place to start.

Do I need a ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro subscription?

No, and those subscriptions don't include an API key anyway. You need a developer API key from the provider's console. The app links straight to each one.

Will it ever send an email on my behalf?

No. Vanilla prepares the email and resume. You choose to save it to Gmail drafts, open it in Gmail, or open it in your mail app. Its Gmail permission can create drafts only, so it can't send.

Why does Google say the app isn't verified?

The first time you save to Gmail drafts, Google shows a warning because the app hasn't been through Google's verification review. The permission it asks for can only create drafts. If you'd rather skip it, use "Open in Gmail", which needs no permission at all.

Will it make up experience to get me through?

No. The fit check and resume only use what's in your profile. Bullets are reordered and emphasised for the role, never invented. A mismatch is called a mismatch.

Does it work with LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed…?

Any screenshot of a job post works. LinkedIn posts with a visible "share your resume at…" line work best, because the recruiter's email is right there.

What happens to my data?

Your profile and applications are stored against your Google account so they sync across devices. Provider keys are encrypted at rest. You can delete your account and everything in it from the Profile screen. Privacy policy · Terms.
